## Who we are

Booster. A small AI-native agency from Wrocław. We build custom B2B software: sales platforms, automations, CRM and ERP implementations, and deployments and configuration of large language models.

Two of our own systems in production. Several client platforms live. Another one in the works.

**The most important thing to know about us: we build with agents and automate our own work.** Claude, OpenCode and Codex are everyday tools for us, not experiments, not marketing copy. We design repositories for agent-driven work: spec-first, strict TypeScript, tests that actually mean something, tooling that lets one person do the work of five.

This isn’t about cutting headcount. It’s the company model we want to build: a small team of seniors delivering what a 50-person agency would. No management layers. No new hire for every new project.

Every process that repeats itself a third time is a candidate for automation. Delivery included.

We’re looking for a founding member, not an employee. Someone who joins our founding team and helps shape it from the inside. Over the next 2-3 years, we’re scaling at least tenfold.

## Why this role

Sales is moving faster than delivery. That’s a good problem, but it is a problem.

We have more projects than bandwidth to close them. Someone needs to take ownership of making sure what we promised a client actually lands in production and works, using the company’s resources and with the support of collaborators.

We’re looking for a co-founder, not an employee. Someone who joins our organisation and helps it grow, so that over the next 2-3 years we scale at least 10x.

## What you’re responsible for

- **End-to-end delivery.** From signed contract to a working system at the client’s. Your scope, your deadline, your call on what to cut to make it.
- **The delivery process.** Definition of done, work standards, metrics, early signals that a project is starting to slip. Right now that’s gut feeling across a few people. With a dozen projects running, gut feeling stops being enough.
- **Bringing order to what’s already in flight.** Our projects are at various stages: some in production, some just past the foundation. You open an unfamiliar repo, figure out where the problem is, and design the next steps. Scope, sequencing, release plan, clear ownership split.
- **Client management.** Relationship, spotting growth areas, upsell, deepening the partnerships we already have. You understand what the client actually needs, can assess how hard it is, and say no in a way that leaves you with a better relationship than before the meeting.
- **Estimates and realistic commitments.** We work fixed-price and T&M. If something can’t be done in six weeks, we want to know before the contract is signed, not in week five.
- **Hands in the code.** We don’t expect you to be coding most of the time, but reviews, trickier sections, and bug fixes are yours. After testing a feature, you fix what can be fixed instead of filing a ticket and waiting for someone. The more independently, the better.
- **Goal for year one:** delivery that scales without adding headcount. A repeatable process, measurable timelines, clients who come back. You define the metrics with us, then they’re yours.
